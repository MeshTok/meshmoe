Privacy

MeshMoe is built so that the default answer to "where is my data?" is on my own machine.

What stays on your machine

  • Local inference never leaves your computer. When you use the bundled MeshMoe-4B model through DSH, prompts and replies are processed by llama-server bound to 127.0.0.1.
  • Mesh task payloads: when your node serves a community task, that task's content passes through the task channel to your machine so it can be computed. It is not stored as training data by default.

What the network stores

  • A node identity: an anonymous node_id and a hash of its token (the token itself lives in ~/.meshmoe/node.json on your machine, not on our servers).
  • Hardware class (CPU / GPU model, RAM) to route suitable tasks, and the region reported by the node.
  • An append-only Joule ledger: energy contributed per task, never subtracted, never transferable. It is public — that is the point of an honor system.

Accounts and claiming

Claiming a node binds an account name to its honor history. Today accounts are email-style identifiers with no password; we therefore recommend claiming only with an address you control, and we are adding verified login before claiming is promoted. Claiming is irreversible: honor cannot be transferred.

Training data

Contributing task trajectories to train future models is opt-in, off by default, revocable at any time, with published sanitization standards. Until you explicitly enable it, no trajectory is collected for training.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/MeshTok/meshmoe.
