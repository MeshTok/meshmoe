Terms

MeshMoe is an open, honor-driven volunteer compute network. By running the node you agree to the following.

Joules are not money

Joules are honor, not currency. They are non-consumable, non-transferable, non-exchangeable, and can never be redeemed for cash, tokens, credits, or goods. They are a permanent public record of energy you contributed. Nothing in the product creates a wallet, balance, or payment obligation.

No service guarantee

The network is experimental community infrastructure. Availability, task assignment, and model quality are provided as-is, without warranty. Local inference on your own machine is always free.

Acceptable use

  • Run the node only on machines you own or are authorized to use.
  • Do not attempt to forge Joules, tokens, node identities, or task results. Cheating results in a public zeroing of the offending record.
  • Task content may be sensitive; do not redistribute it.

Open source

The node, server, site, and DSH plugin are Apache-2.0. MeshMoe-4B derives from open Qwen3.5 / NeoHorse-1 lineage. You can inspect exactly what the node does.

Changes

These terms evolve with the project. Material changes are announced in the repository and on the site.
