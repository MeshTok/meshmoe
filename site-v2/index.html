FAQ

Is it really free? How do you survive?

Local inference is free forever — it runs on your own machine and costs us nothing. Idle compute for community tasks is donated voluntarily (in exchange for Joules). Future revenue comes from enterprise APIs and optional paid capacity for users without capable hardware — community members never pay. We don't sell data, and honor is never for sale.

Can Joules be exchanged for money?

No. Never. Joules are honor, not currency — non-consumable, non-transferable, non-exchangeable. That's exactly why they stay clean: no financial properties, no arbitrage, no incentive to farm.

Will you steal my data?

Local inference data never leaves your machine — 100%. Task content from mesh jobs lives only in the task channel. Training-data contribution is opt-in (off by default), revocable anytime, with fully published sanitization standards.

My machine is old. Can I still join?

Yes. MeshMoe-4B runs CPU-only with 4 GB RAM — slower, but you still burn Joules. Got a 24 GB GPU? Install the tower tier (Qwen3.8-27B), take harder tasks, burn faster.

Why "MeshMoe"?

Mesh + MoE (Mixture of Experts). In a model-level MoE, a router sends each token to the most qualified expert sub-network. MeshMoe scales that to the network level — the router sends each task to the most qualified machine. A million computers, one Mixture-of-Experts brain.
